# Vision

Proust Club is a reading companion for _À la recherche du temps perdu_.

_À la recherche du temps perdu_ is filled with extraordinary passages — sentences worth keeping, revisiting and seeing in a new light. Proust Club helps you do exactly that.

It helps readers capture memorable passages, organize their discoveries and deepen their understanding of one of literature's richest works.

Proust Club adds a new layer to the reading experience, helping readers organize the novel, connect its ideas and discover new perspectives over time.

**Ultimately, Proust Club helps readers keep the conversation with the novel alive long after the last page.**

## Core experience

Find a passage you remember — or one worth preserving. Isolate it from the surrounding text, save it, annotate it and organize it in your own collection. Over time, your collection become a personal map of your reading.

## Progression

**Start with individual reading:** collect, annotate, organize and revisit.

**Later grow into a shared experience:** collections, recommendations and conversations around the text.
